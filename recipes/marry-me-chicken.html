The Original Marry Me Chicken

Description

"Marry Me" Chicken is a classic dish that's simple to make and guaranteed to please. Starting from a basic grilled chicken breast, the creamy sauce brings complex and pleasing Tuscan flavors that are easy to throw together for a winning comfort meal any day of the week!

Ingredients

  • 4 Chicken breasts
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2 Cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • Thyme, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon dried red pepper flakes
  • 3/4 cup chickken broth
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup fresh grated parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Sear chicken in 1 tbsp olive oil in an oven-proof skillet on the stove. Remove from pan and set aside
  2. In the same pan add remaining 2 tbsp olive oil and saute garlic, red pepper and thyme until lightly toasted
  3. Stir in heavy cream, broth, sun-dried tomatoes, and parmesan. Simmer and reduce.
  4. Return chicken to pan and move to oven. Bake at 375 degrees F for 10-12 minutes, or until chicken is cooked to an internal temperature of 165 degrees F
