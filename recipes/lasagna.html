The Best Homemade Lasagna Recipe

Description

This is a basic lasagna recipe that will help you create a delicious dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 box oven-ready lasagna noodles
  • 1 jar pasta sauce
  • 1 lb ground italian sausage
  • 1 container ricotta cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F
  2. In a frying pan, cook sausage until browned and drain grease
  3. Add pasta sauce to pan with sausage and bring to a simmer
  4. Cover bottom of 9"x12" baking dish with thin layer of sauce
  5. Spread a thin layer of ricotta cheese on noodles and arrange in single layer covering bottom of dish
  6. Cover noodle layer with a thin layer of meat sauce
  7. Repeat for 5 layers
  8. Evenly sprinkle shredded mozzarella over the top of the lasagna
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes
  10. Remove foil and bake for another 5 minutes or until cheese is brown
  11. After removing from oven, let stand several minutes to cool down before serving
